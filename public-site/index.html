Experiments you can compare, not just record.

BioMat Lab keeps each project's own variables, knows which of them are conditions and which are outcomes, and refuses to compare two numbers whose definitions differ. It is topic-agnostic: a DES lignin extraction and a protein purification project share the engine.

Interpretation

ObservedThe run that heated first and milled afterwards recovered more lignin: 2.30 % vs 0.96 % of dry biomass.
Possible explanationsHeating may dissolve lignin before mechanical treatment, exposing fresh surface; or milling for the whole period may shorten contact with the liquid phase.
Alternative explanationsRun-to-run variation (no replicates were recorded), or a different local temperature at the sample despite the same set point.
LimitsTwo single runs, no replicate statistics. A causal claim is not supported.

02Scientific comparability

Two numbers are compared only after their definitions are compared. The verdict is one of four states, and it is shown before any difference is calculated.

Yield and recovery are not the same measurement

Lignin yield derived

recovered lignin dry biomass

388 mg ÷ 3 000 mg = 12.93 %

How much of the wood became lignin product.

Lignin recovery derived

recovered lignin lignin present (Klason)

388 mg ÷ 660 mg = 58.8 %

How much of the lignin in the wood was extracted.

The same 388 mg gives two different numbers. A comparison between a yield and a recovery is reported as ✕ not comparable, with both definitions shown and a conversion requested before any difference is computed.
